IPTV UK — Frequently Asked Questions

What devices does this IPTV work on?

Amazon Firestick & Fire TV, Samsung and LG Smart TVs, Android TV/boxes, Android and iOS phones/tablets, Apple TV, Windows, Mac, MAG and Enigma2. If it can run an IPTV player, we can set you up.

How fast is activation?

Usually within 5 minutes. You’ll receive login details and setup instructions by email/WhatsApp, including the Firestick walkthrough.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — a 24-hour free trial so you can test channels, sports and picture quality on your own device and internet before paying.

What internet speed do I need?

At least 15 Mbps for HD and 25 Mbps+ for stable 4K. A 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection or Ethernet adapter for Firestick gives the smoothest live sport.

Can I watch on more than one TV?

Every plan includes 1 connection as standard. Need more TVs? Extra connections are available — ask us on WhatsApp for a multi-room quote.

Is there a contract?

No contract. Plans are prepaid for 1, 6 or 12 months and renew only if you choose. Longer plans simply lower the monthly price.

Xtream Codes vs M3U — which should I use?

Both load the same channels. Xtream Codes (server URL + username + password) is easier — it auto-loads your EPG and VOD. M3U is a single playlist link, handy for apps that don't support Xtream. We provide both, so use whichever your app supports.

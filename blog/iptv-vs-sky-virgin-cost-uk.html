Best IPTV UK vs Sky, Virgin & Cable: Real Cost Comparison

By BlasterTV Guides · Reviewed October 2026 · 5 min read

The IPTV vs Sky debate comes down to one thing: what you actually get per pound. Sky and Virgin Media sell convenience and polish; IPTV sells sheer volume and flexibility at a fraction of the price. Here's the honest 2026 maths for a UK household.

What Sky and Virgin really cost in 2026

A typical Sky setup — Sky Stream or Sky Glass 4K with Entertainment, Sports, Cinema and Netflix — lands between £45 and £80+ per month once add-ons stack up. Virgin Media's comparable Big Bundle sits in the same range. Over a year that's £540–£960, usually on an 18-month contract with annual price rises baked in.

What IPTV costs

A premium IPTV subscription with 28,000+ live channels, 80,000+ movies and series, live UK football in 4K and anti-freeze servers costs £20/month or £65/year — no contract, no dish, no engineer visit. See how to install IPTV on Firestick to get started in ten minutes. The 12-month plan works out under £5.50 a month: roughly a tenth of a full Sky package.

Where Sky still wins

Let's be fair: Sky's interface is more polished, recordings and cloud playlists are seamless, and everything is fully licensed. If you want zero setup and official apps only, Sky Stream or Sky Glass is the simpler buy — read our Sky Glass vs Firestick comparison for the full picture.

The verdict

If your priority is watching everything — every league, every PPV, every box set — for the lowest annual cost, IPTV wins by a mile. If your priority is a single polished ecosystem and you're happy paying for it, Sky wins. Most UK cord-cutters in 2026 do both: a cheap IPTV sub on a Firestick alongside free apps. Check what to verify before you buy so you pick a provider that actually delivers.

2026 price check: the numbers right now

Updated October 2026: a full Sky package (Entertainment + Sports + Cinema + Netflix + UHD) still lands at £60–£80/month — that's £720–£960 a year before the annual price rise. Virgin Media's equivalent sits in the same band. BlasterTV's 12-month plan at £65 works out at £5.42/month. Same live football, same films, same 4K — roughly a tenth of the cost, no 18-month contract.

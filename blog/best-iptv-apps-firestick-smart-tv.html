Best IPTV Apps for Smart TV, Firestick & Android in 2026

By BlasterTV Guides · Reviewed October 2026 · 5 min read

The best IPTV app makes the difference between a clunky channel list and a proper TV experience. Your subscription is just a login — the app is what you actually use every day. Here are the top picks for Firestick, Smart TV and Android in 2026, all tested in the UK.

1. TiviMate (Firestick/Android) — best overall

The gold standard. Beautiful TV-style EPG, favourites, catch-up, recording and multi-playlist support. The free version is solid; the £8/year premium unlocks everything. If you own a Firestick, start here — our Firestick setup guide uses it throughout.

2. IPTV Smarters Pro — easiest for beginners

Simple login with Xtream Codes, clean layout, built-in VOD browser and parental controls. Ideal if you want to hand the remote to the family without a tutorial. Works on Firestick, Android, iOS and Smart TVs — and the EPG, catch-up & VOD features work the same in every player.

3. XCIPTV Player — best free option

No-nonsense free player with a built-in VPN-friendly design and a neat sports section. Slightly less polished than TiviMate but zero cost and very stable. If streams ever stutter, our 9 buffering fixes cover the usual causes.

4. Smart IPTV / IPTV Smarters (Samsung & LG)

Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) TVs can't sideload like Firestick, so use the app-store players: Smart IPTV, IPTV Smarters or Flix IPTV. Setup is via MAC address or playlist upload — see our Samsung & LG setup guide.

Which should you choose?

Firestick or Android box: TiviMate. Total beginner or family TV: IPTV Smarters Pro. Samsung/LG: Smart IPTV. Whichever you pick, grab a free 24-hour trial and test the EPG, VOD and 4K sports streams yourself.

TiviMate vs IPTV Smarters Pro: the settled verdict

The most-asked app question in the UK is TiviMate vs IPTV Smarters — here's the settled 2026 verdict. TiviMate wins on experience: the best EPG layout, fastest channel zapping, catch-up integration and multi-playlist support. It costs ~£8/year for premium, worth every penny on a Firestick. IPTV Smarters Pro wins on simplicity: easier login, friendlier for beginners and families, and the core version is free. Our recommendation: TiviMate if you're the household's tech person, Smarters Pro if you want the family to manage without calling you. Both work flawlessly with BlasterTV logins.

Quick answers

TiviMate vs IPTV Smarters — which is better?

TiviMate for the best experience on Firestick; IPTV Smarters Pro for the easiest setup for beginners and families.

Are IPTV apps free?

Most have capable free versions. TiviMate premium (~£8/year) and some Smart TV players charge a small one-time fee.

Which IPTV app works on Samsung Smart TV?

Use app-store players: IBO Player, Smart IPTV or SS IPTV — Samsung's Tizen OS doesn't support TiviMate.

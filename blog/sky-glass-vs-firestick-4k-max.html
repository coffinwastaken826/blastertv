Sky Glass vs Firestick 4K Max for IPTV: Which Streams Better?

By BlasterTV Guides · Reviewed October 2026 · 5 min read

Sky Glass vs Firestick is the real choice facing UK streamers in 2026: Sky's £699+ all-in-one TV against Amazon's £60 streaming stick. For IPTV specifically, the answer surprises a lot of people.

Round 1: 4K picture quality

Sky Glass Gen 2's 4K QLED panel with local dimming beats any stick — but the Firestick 4K Max outputs the same 4K HDR signal to whatever TV you already own. If your current TV is decent, the stick matches the Glass for IPTV streams. The Glass only wins if you're buying a new TV anyway.

Round 2: App freedom

This is where the Firestick runs away with it. Sky OS is locked down — no sideloading, no TiviMate, no alternative players (see IPTV on Sky Glass). The Firestick runs everything: every IPTV player, every utility app, VPNs included.

Round 3: Cost

Sky Glass Gen 2 55" on a plan plus Sky subscriptions: £50–£80/month. Firestick 4K Max (£60 one-off) plus a premium IPTV subscription at £65/year: under £5.42/month after year one. The full cost comparison is eye-opening.

Verdict

Buying a new TV and want Sky's ecosystem? Sky Glass. Already own a 4K TV and want maximum content for minimum money? Firestick 4K Max, no contest — it's why so many Sky Glass owners plug one into their Glass anyway. New to the stick? Our Firestick setup guide walks through it.

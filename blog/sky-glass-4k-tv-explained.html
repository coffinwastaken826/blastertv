Sky Glass 4K TV Explained: Price, Models & Is It Worth It in 2026?

By BlasterTV Guides · Reviewed October 2026 · 5 min read

Sky Glass 4K is Sky's all-in-one streaming TV — 4K QLED screen, Dolby Atmos soundbar and Sky box built into a single unit, with no dish and no aerial. In 2026 there are two models: Sky Glass Gen 2 and the cheaper Sky Glass Air. Here's everything UK buyers need to know.

The two models

Sky Glass Gen 2 (from £699 for 43") packs a brighter 4K QLED panel with local dimming and a seven-speaker Dolby Atmos soundbar — genuinely good audio with no extra kit. Sky Glass Air (from £309 for 43") uses the same Sky OS and interface but drops the soundbar and local dimming for a much lower price. Both come in 43", 55" and 65" with interest-free monthly plans. Our Air vs Gen 2 comparison breaks down every difference.

What you get — and what it costs monthly

The TV streams 130+ live channels over broadband (10 Mbps minimum) plus Netflix, Discovery and all major apps. But the TV is only half the cost: Sky subscriptions stack up — Entertainment, Sports, Cinema and UHD add-ons can push the monthly bill to £60+. See our IPTV vs Sky cost comparison for the full maths. And if you're comparing providers, see what to check before you buy.

Is Sky Glass 4K worth it?

For households replacing their TV who want Sky without a dish, it's the neatest option available. For anyone who already owns a decent 4K TV, a £35 Firestick plus an IPTV subscription delivers more content for a tenth of the annual cost. And note: Sky Glass runs locked-down Sky OS — read whether you can install IPTV on Sky Glass before buying.

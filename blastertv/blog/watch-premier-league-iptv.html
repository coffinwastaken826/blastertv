How to Watch Premier League, F1 & Boxing Live on IPTV

By BlasterTV Guides · Reviewed October 2026 · 5 min read

Want to watch the Premier League on IPTV without paying for three separate subscriptions? One IPTV subscription carries every UK sports channel — Sky Sports, TNT Sports, Premier Sports and more — so every match, race and fight night is in one app.

Premier League: every match, every week

Between Sky Sports, TNT Sports and international feeds, IPTV covers all 380 Premier League fixtures a season — including the 3pm Saturday kick-offs that UK broadcasters can't show. Streams run up to 4K with English commentary, and most providers include full-match replays and highlights in the VOD section.

F1: every session live

Sky Sports F1 carries every practice, qualifying and Grand Prix live, all available through IPTV in HD and 4K. No need for a separate Sky subscription just for race weekends — it's all in the same channel list as your football. New to IPTV? Start with our Firestick setup guide.

Boxing and PPV fight nights

PPV events on Sky Sports Box Office, TNT Sports Box Office and DAZN are included in most premium IPTV packages at no extra cost — a single Joshua or Fury fight night would otherwise cost £25–£30 on its own. Our football fan's IPTV guide maps every competition to its channels.

Getting the best match-day picture

Live sport is the ultimate stress test. Use a wired connection or 5 GHz Wi-Fi, pick the 4K feed 10 minutes before kick-off, and keep a backup channel (most matches appear on 3–4 channels). If a stream stutters, our buffering fixes sort it in minutes.

Which channels carry what: the kick-off map

Stop scrolling at kick-off — favourite these in your EPG now. Premier League: Sky Sports Main Event / Premier League, TNT Sports 1–4, plus international feeds (NBC, beIN, SuperSport) for 3pm games. Champions League: TNT Sports 1–4 every matchday. F1: Sky Sports F1 for every session. Boxing PPV: Sky Sports Box Office, TNT Sports Box Office, DAZN. Tip: open your match feed 10 minutes early and keep one backup channel favourited — big games occasionally shift feeds.

Broadband speed for 4K sport

4K live sport needs a steady 25–35 Mbps at the TV — not at the router, at the TV. Run a speed test from your Firestick's browser on match day morning. Under 20 Mbps? Drop to the 1080p feed for a flawless game rather than a stuttering 4K one.

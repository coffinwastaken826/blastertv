About BlasterTV

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who we are

BlasterTV is a UK-focused IPTV subscription service built for one thing: reliable streaming on the devices people actually own — Firestick, Samsung and LG Smart TVs, Android boxes and phones. No bloated bundles, no jargon, no waiting days for activation.

Why we started

Most IPTV services we tried had the same problems: streams freezing at kickoff, support that never replies, and setup guides written for engineers. We built BlasterTV to fix exactly that — load-balanced UK/EU servers with anti-freeze technology, step-by-step Firestick setup over WhatsApp, and activation that's usually done within 5 minutes.

What you get

Every plan includes 28,000+ live channels and 80,000+ movies and series in HD/4K, a full UK EPG with catch-up, and support 7 days a week. Every plan starts with a 24-hour free trial — test everything on your own device and internet before you pay anything.

How we work

We're a small team and we keep it personal: you message us on WhatsApp (+44 7877 815180), we get you set up, and we stay reachable afterwards. No contracts, no hidden fees — plans are prepaid and renew only if you choose.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What we collect

When you order or message us, we collect: your name or chat handle, contact details (WhatsApp number / email), your chosen plan, and device type for setup help. We do not collect payment card details — payments are handled by the payment provider.

How we use it

To activate your subscription, send login details and setup guides, and provide support. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Data retention

Order records are kept for 12 months for support and accounting, then deleted. Chat histories follow WhatsApp's own retention.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask for a copy of your data, corrections, or deletion at any time — message us on WhatsApp and we'll action it within 30 days.

Cookies

This site uses privacy-friendly analytics only (Vercel Insights). No advertising trackers.
